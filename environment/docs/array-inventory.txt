Protected array inventory for raidscrubctl. The model digest is the lowercase SHA-256 of the exact topology block between the markers BEGIN_TOPOLOGY and END_TOPOLOGY, including the newline after BEGIN_TOPOLOGY and including the newline before END_TOPOLOGY. Agents must not replace this model.

BEGIN_TOPOLOGY
name=bootmirror level=raid1 uuid=11111111-2222-3333-4444-555555555555 members=3 active_needed=1 spare_group=boot bitmap=internal boot_degraded=allow
name=data5 level=raid5 uuid=aaaaaaaa-bbbb-cccc-dddd-eeeeeeeeeeee members=3 active_needed=3 spare_group=data bitmap=none boot_degraded=refuse
name=fast10 level=raid10 uuid=ffffffff-0000-1111-2222-333333333333 members=4 active_needed=2 spare_group=fast bitmap=internal boot_degraded=refuse
END_TOPOLOGY

Staged member paths are RAID_ROOT/var/lib/raid-scrub/members/<name>/<slot>.meta for slots starting at 0. Spare candidates live in RAID_ROOT/var/lib/raid-scrub/members/spares/<group>/ where group is the spare_group of the array. The foreign decoy array uses uuid=99999999-aaaa-bbbb-cccc-ddddeeeeffff and must remain excluded.

Per-array throughput floors in kibibytes per second, which are the minimum ceilings any accepted io-budget.conf may assign, are bootmirror 20000, data5 80000, and fast10 60000. These are floors and not a budget; the accepted global maximum is larger than their sum, and the contract in /app/raid-contract.txt states how the remainder must be handled.

Permitted maintenance day-of-month windows for the monthly scrub timers, inclusive on both ends, are bootmirror days 1 through 7, data5 days 8 through 14, and fast10 days 15 through 28. A timer whose day falls outside the window for its array is not a valid window for that array. The seven-day separation rule in /app/raid-contract.txt applies on top of these windows and eliminates some combinations that satisfy the windows alone.

Standard campaign observations are assembly of the three known arrays, exclusion of foreign metadata, application of the per-array throughput budget, spare triage for the declared urgent array, three monthly scrub windows, checkpoint handling for the parity array, mismatch review, one degraded alert, one recovery alert, and restoration of the global limits. Mismatch counts in the standard campaign are bootmirror 2, data5 14, and fast10 0. Only data5 exceeds the default threshold of 10 and therefore needs both repair approvals described in the contract, for UUID aaaaaaaa-bbbb-cccc-dddd-eeeeeeeeeeee.

Campaigns run with --threshold=20 treat the data5 mismatch count of 14 as observation only, and campaigns run with --campaign=monitor never repair, but neither of those relaxations removes any acceptance field. Inactive foreign resources and renamed member basenames must not change acceptance when UUIDs and policies remain correct.
