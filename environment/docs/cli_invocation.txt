Controller invocations used by the programme office
---------------------------------------------------

Build the controller:

    make -C /app/environment all

Full replay across every hall in the published index:

    /app/environment/bin/certctl --all --out /app/output/certification_report.json

Single hall replay:

    /app/environment/bin/certctl --site sr_arden --out /app/output/one_site.json

The Makefile `report` target wraps the full replay with the same destination.

Verifier pytest flags: --ctrf /logs/verifier/ctrf.json
