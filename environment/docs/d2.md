# Operator notes

`scripts/s1.sh` is the only publish that counts for handover. Pulse (`cmd/a2.sh`) is a local convenience check used during bring-up; treat disagreements between pulse and probe records as unresolved until `s1` converges.

Profiles `north` and `south` load rank boosts from `fixtures/p1/`. Case ids are listed in `docs/d3.txt`. Scratch under `/app/environment/scratch` holds intermediate tables; do not treat scratch alone as the deliverable.

`scripts/s2.sh` is intentionally destructive and plants more than one durable marker plus a poison generation journal. If durable material looks poisoned after an ops drill, use `scripts/s3.sh` before expecting a clean terminal seal from `s1`. Warm flags under `scratch/warm/` are not a substitute for recomputed remount entries, and remount `gen_epoch` must track the active book generation across republishes.
