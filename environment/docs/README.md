This benchmark is a held-out item-choice problem built from the Open Bandit Dataset small release. Its event contexts, campaign catalogs, logger identities, candidate sets, and disclosed product attributes come from that source. Choice outcomes are semi-synthetic so that transferable demand and logger exposure are independently identifiable and auditable.

Training events expose the observed product and its behavior-policy propensity. Evaluation events use later calendar days and conceal the product. Campaign names indicate the all-products, men's, and women's source catalogs. Logger names distinguish the random and Bernoulli Thompson Sampling arms.
