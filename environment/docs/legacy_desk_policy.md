# Retired desk procedure

Before the controller merge each desk kept its own spreadsheet and mailed a
tally to the programme office on Fridays. The office reconciled the seven
tallies by hand and published a pack on Monday.

Desks disagreed often enough that the reconciliation step regularly slipped into
Tuesday, which is what motivated folding the seven tools into one controller.

This document is retained for audit history only. The published contract in
`certification_contract.md` supersedes it.
