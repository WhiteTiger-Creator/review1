The target is the chosen item conditional on an event's disclosed ten-product set. Training choices were observed through one of two behavior loggers. Correct their unequal exposure with the reciprocal of behavior_propensity, capped at ten.

Evaluation events occur after all training events. Scoring applies the same capped exposure weighting and checks predictive quality overall and within every campaign, logger, and capped or uncapped exposure stratum. It also checks a cold-start copy in which evaluation product identifiers are replaced but the disclosed product features are preserved.

Identifiers and relation order carry no signal. Verification can consistently rename event keys, replace evaluation-only item keys, and reorder any relation. Attach scores by event_id and item_id and normalize within each candidate set. A model therefore needs transferable item effects and interactions with campaign, position, and user context rather than an identifier-frequency table.
