60 - THE CLOSING SHEET                            C147 - C160
==============================================================

C147  --selfcheck. Run with that one flag the referee settles the crown, files the closing sheet,
      settles it a second time and files it again, and prints nothing at all on standard output or
      standard error. It proves ONE THING ONLY: that a repeat run agrees with itself. IT NEVER
      PROVES THAT ANY RULING IS RIGHT. A referee that reads every rung the wrong way round agrees
      with itself perfectly and files the same wrong sheet twice.

C148  THE SHEET IS FILED AT THE FULL PATH NAMED BY CLOSING_SHEET, and where that names nothing at
      /app/var/lib/hallowspar/closing-sheet.txt. Any directory on that path that does not exist is
      made. The sheet is one plain text file and there is no other output of any kind.
C149  THE HEAD LINE is the first line of the sheet and reads, with exactly one blank between fields:
        bracket <name> berths <n> houses <n> rounds <n> boards <n> byes <n> concedes <n>
      <name> is the crown's name from crown.conf. `berths` counts the lines of bracket.table and
      `houses` the lines of roll.table. `rounds` is THE ROUND THE RECORD STANDS IN WHEN IT ENDS,
      counting from one and moved on by every close (C124), so a record holding eight closes reads
      rounds 9. `boards` counts the boards that were WEIGHED, `byes` the byes that were weighed and
      `concedes` the concessions that were weighed; a step that reached nothing (C034) is counted in
      none of the three.
C150  THE BERTH BLOCK HEADER is the second line of the sheet and reads exactly:
        -- berths --
C151  THE BERTH LINES follow, ONE FOR EVERY BERTH bracket.table names, no more and no fewer. Each is
      written with exactly one blank between fields, in this order:
        the berth's name             padded on the RIGHT to 9
        the word `house`
        the house it seats at the close, or a dash where it seats none, padded on the RIGHT to 9
        the word `boards`
        the count of boards weighed at that berth, padded on the LEFT to 2
        the word `hands`
        the count of hands taken at that berth, padded on the LEFT to 3
        the berth's state            padded on the RIGHT to 8
        the berth's rule token       padded on the RIGHT to 15
      A field longer than its width is written whole and is never cut. Blanks left at the END of a
      line by this padding are removed, so no line ends in a blank.
C152  THE BERTH BLOCK IS SORTED ASCENDING BY BERTH NAME, comparing the names byte by byte from the
      left. IT IS NOT IN BRACKET ORDER: bracket order is the reseat order and nothing else (C005,
      C006).
C153  THE HOUSE BLOCK HEADER follows the last berth line and reads exactly:
        -- houses --
C154  THE HOUSE LINES follow, ONE FOR EVERY HOUSE roll.table names, no more and no fewer. Each is
      written with exactly one blank between fields, in this order:
        the house's name             padded on the RIGHT to 9
        the word `seed`      then its opening seed,                    padded on the LEFT to 2
        the word `won`       then its wins,                            padded on the LEFT to 2
        the word `lost`      then its losses,                          padded on the LEFT to 2
        the word `met`       then its count of meetings,               padded on the LEFT to 2
        the word `strength`  then its strength read at the close,      padded on the LEFT to 2
        the word `for`       then the hands it took,                   padded on the LEFT to 3
        the word `against`   then the hands it gave,                   padded on the LEFT to 3
        the word `berth`     then the berth it sits in at the close, or a dash where it sits in
                             none,                                     padded on the RIGHT to 9
        the word `place`     then its place,                           padded on the LEFT to 2
        its place token              padded on the RIGHT to 14
        its state                    padded on the RIGHT to 8
        its rule token               padded on the RIGHT to 15
      A field longer than its width is written whole and is never cut, and blanks left at the end of
      a line are removed.
C155  THE HOUSE BLOCK IS IN ROLL ORDER, top line of roll.table first (C012). It is not sorted by
      seed, by place, by state or by name.
C156  THE LAST TWO LINES ARE THE TALLIES, each written with exactly one blank between fields:
        berths CROWNED <n> UPSET <n> RESTED <n> VACATED <n> DARK <n>
        houses CROWNED <n> BARRED <n> CUT <n> FELLED <n> SEATED <n>
      The states stand in the order the closed sets are stated in (C127, C136) and EVERY STATE IS
      PRINTED EVEN WHERE ITS COUNT IS NOTHING. The berth counts add up to the number of berths and
      the house counts to the number of houses.
C157  THE SHEET ENDS WITH EXACTLY ONE NEWLINE and carries no blank line anywhere. It carries NO SEAL
      OF ANY KIND: no digest, no hash, no checksum, no chained line and no count of its own bytes.
      Its length is fixed by the tables alone at five lines plus one for every berth and one for
      every house, and no record however long or short moves that count.
C158  THE RUNNING DISCIPLINE. A run that files a sheet prints NOTHING on standard output and nothing
      on standard error. The recorded crown under RECORD_ROOT is read and never written to, and so
      are the laws. Two runs over an unchanged record file the same bytes. The referee reads no
      network, no clock and no source of chance.

C159  THE AWKWARD CASES OF THE RECORD, gathered again so nothing has to be hunted for:
        a step naming a berth or a house the tables do not name reaches nothing, takes no line and
          is counted in no column (C032, C033, C034);
        a seat naming a house already seated, a house already out, or a berth already seating a
          house reaches nothing, but the berth is NAMED, so it reads dark.named (C038 to C043);
        a board or a concession naming a berth that seats nobody, or one whose house is out,
          reaches nothing (C045, C067);
        a board or a concession naming the same berth twice reaches nothing (C046, C067);
        a board on which both took the same hands falls to the WORSE opening seed, and both hands
          still enter both records (C048);
        a board of no hands at all on either side is weighed the same way (C049);
        a bye when no house is seated reaches nothing (C060);
        a bye may fall on a house that has already drawn one, and both wins count (C061);
        a strike naming a house already out, or not on the roll, changes nothing (C070);
        a strike of a house standing in no berth puts it out barred.aside and vacates no berth
          (C134, C138);
        a strike after the house has taken the crown leaves it CROWNED (C123, C136).

C160  THE AWKWARD CASES OF THE CLOSE AND THE SHEET, gathered again:
        a close where the width is at or above the number seated puts nobody out and STILL reseats
          every survivor into the berths the bracket names first (C104);
        a close with no house seated at all still advances the round (C105);
        a close asking for a round past the last width declared holds the last width (C010, C106);
        a close leaving exactly one house seated crowns it, crown.width where that close put
          somebody out and crown.sole where it did not (C121, C122);
        a house put out that stood in no board in the round just closed is cut.idle, unbeaten in
          that round whatever it lost in earlier ones (C111);
        a house that lost boards in earlier rounds and won the one board of the round just closed
          is cut.width and NOT felled (C112);
        a house that both gave a board up and lost another in the round just closed is felled.given
          (C109);
        a house the record never seats is SEATED with stand.unseated, and no close reaches it
          (C090, C141);
        a house struck at its peak keeps the place its frozen standing earns, so it can place above
          houses that survived four more rounds (C094, C143);
        a berth whose house a reseating moved on, and which nothing seated again, is VACATED with
          void.reseated (C118, C134);
        a berth still seating a house at the close, holding neither crown nor upset nor bye, is
          VACATED with void.standing (C134), which is what a record ending in mid round leaves;
        a record with no steps at all leaves every berth DARK with dark.silent, every house SEATED
          with stand.unseated, places settled on the opening seeds with the WORSE seed placed
          first, and every tally row printed at its zero (C127, C136, C143, C156).
