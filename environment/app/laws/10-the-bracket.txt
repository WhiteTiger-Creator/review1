10 - THE BRACKET                                  C001 - C028
==============================================================

C001  THE RECORDED CROWN is read from the directory named by RECORD_ROOT, and where that names
      nothing the referee reads /app/crown. Four files stand at the top of that directory and one
      more under record/: crown.conf, bracket.table, widths.table, roll.table and
      record/crown.log. Nothing else in that directory is read.
C002  EVERY LINE OF EVERY FILE is read the same way. A line whose first character after any leading
      blanks is a hash is a comment and is passed over. A line holding nothing but blanks is passed
      over. Every other line is split on runs of blanks into fields, and leading and trailing blanks
      are discarded before the split.
C003  crown.conf carries the crown's name on a line reading `name` and then the name. The name is
      printed on the head line of the closing sheet and is read for nothing else.
C004  bracket.table names every BERTH the crown has, one berth to a line, the berth's name being the
      whole of the line.
C005  THE ORDER OF THE LINES OF bracket.table IS BRACKET ORDER. It is the order a reseating fills
      berths in (C115) and it is used for that and for nothing else.
C006  BRACKET ORDER IS NOT THE ORDER THE CLOSING SHEET PRINTS BERTHS IN. The sheet's berth block is
      sorted by berth name (C152). A referee that prints the berths in bracket order has the right
      lines in the wrong order and every one of them is wrong.
C007  The berths the bracket names are all the berths there are. No step of the record can make a
      berth, and no step can take one away. A berth stands before the first step is read and stands
      after the last.
C008  widths.table names, one line to a round, the round number and then the WIDTH that round
      allows. The lines stand in round order, the first line being round one.
C009  THE WIDTH OF A ROUND is how many houses that round allows to stand in a berth. It is read at a
      close, and the width read there is the width of the round the close OPENS, never the width of
      the round it ends (C102).
C010  WHERE A CLOSE ASKS FOR A ROUND PAST THE LAST ROUND widths.table declares, the LAST WIDTH
      DECLARED holds, for that close and for every close after it. The table is never wrapped round
      and no width is ever invented.
C011  roll.table names every HOUSE the crown has, one line to a house, carrying the house's name and
      then its OPENING SEED.
C012  THE ORDER OF THE LINES OF roll.table IS ROLL ORDER, and roll order is the order the closing
      sheet prints houses in (C155). It is not the order of the seeds and it is not the order of the
      places.
C013  AN OPENING SEED is a whole number, and no two houses share one. A house's seed is fixed by the
      table and no step of the record changes it.
C014  A LOWER SEED NUMBER IS A BETTER SEED and a higher seed number is a WORSE SEED. Nothing else in
      these laws reads a seed except the rungs that name it (C048, C086).
C015  The houses the roll names are all the houses there are. A house is on the roll before the
      record opens and is on it when the record closes, whether it took the crown, was struck off,
      was cut, was felled or never stood in a berth at all.
C016  The four tables are read whole, top line first, BEFORE the first step of the record is read.
C017  THE RECORD NEVER GROWS A TABLE. A step naming a berth the bracket does not name, or a house
      the roll does not name, reaches nothing (C032, C033); it does not add either one.
C018  A BERTH SEATS AT MOST ONE HOUSE at a time. While a house stands in a berth no other house can
      be seated there.
C019  A HOUSE STANDS IN AT MOST ONE BERTH at a time. While a house stands in a berth it cannot be
      seated in another.
C020  A house may also stand in NO berth: before it is first seated, after it is put out, and after
      a reseating leaves it no berth. A house standing in no berth is not seated.
C021  record/crown.log carries the record. Every line of it is one step, and the steps are read in
      the order the lines stand (C029).
C022  Hands, wins, losses, meetings, strength, boards and places are WHOLE NUMBERS and are never
      negative. No quantity in these laws is a fraction, a ratio, a rate or a share.
C023  There is no clock anywhere in the crown. A round is a count of closes and never a span of
      time, and no step carries a date, an hour or a duration.
C024  There is nothing uncertain in the crown either. Every board that was played is in the record
      with the hands each side took, and nothing is drawn, guessed or estimated.
C025  A HOUSE NEVER MEETS ITSELF. No board and no concession ever puts a house against itself,
      whatever berths the step names (C046, C067).
C026  THE CROWN IS TAKEN AT MOST ONCE in a record and is never given up once taken (C123).
C027  The number of berths and the number of houses need not agree. There may be more berths than
      houses, more houses than berths, or the same count of each.
C028  THE LAWS STAND OUTSIDE THE RECORD ROOT, under /app/laws, so nothing the bracket, the roll or
      the record could name can reach them. The referee reads them and never writes to them.
