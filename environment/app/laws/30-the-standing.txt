30 - THE STANDING                                 C071 - C098
==============================================================

C071  A STANDING is an order over houses, best first. It is what a bye reads to find the lowest
      house, what a board reads to know whether a berth is UPSET, and what a close reads to know
      who goes out and who sits where next.
C072  THE STANDING IS WEIGHED ON FOUR RUNGS, IN THIS ORDER, THE FIRST THAT SEPARATES DECIDING:
        rung one    more WINS
        rung two    fewer LOSSES
        rung three  more STRENGTH
        rung four   the WORSE OPENING SEED
      A rung on which two houses are level decides nothing between them and the next rung down is
      weighed. No rung is ever weighed before a rung above it.
C073  RUNG ONE IS WINS, AND MORE OF THEM RANKS ABOVE. A house on four wins ranks above a house on
      three, whatever either has done on any other rung.
C074  A WIN is a board taken (C050) or a bye drawn (C058). Those are the only two things that enter
      a win and they are worth exactly the same.
C075  A CONCEDED BOARD ENTERS NO WIN AT ALL. The house the board was given up to gains nothing on
      rung one by it (C064).
C076  RUNG TWO IS LOSSES, AND FEWER OF THEM RANKS ABOVE. It is weighed only where two houses hold
      the same count of wins.
C077  A LOSS is a board lost (C050) or a board given up (C064). A house that gives a board up takes
      the loss for it; the house it was given up to takes nothing.
C078  RUNG THREE IS STRENGTH, AND MORE OF IT RANKS ABOVE. It is weighed only where two houses hold
      the same count of wins and the same count of losses.
C079  A HOUSE'S STRENGTH IS THE WINS STANDING TO THE HOUSES IT HAS MET. Take the houses it has met
      at a board or at a concession (C053, C065), read the wins each of them stands on, and add
      them together. A house that has met nobody has a strength of nothing.
C080  EVERY MEETING IS COUNTED SEPARATELY. Where a house has met the same house three times, that
      house's wins are added three times.
C081  STRENGTH IS READ AT THE MOMENT IT IS WEIGHED and is never carried forward from an earlier
      reading. It is not a running total kept in a house's own record, it is not entered when a
      board is played, and it cannot be cached. Every standing reads it again from the meetings.
C082  SO A HOUSE'S STRENGTH MOVES WITHOUT THE HOUSE PLAYING. A house that met an opponent in round
      one sees its own strength rise every time that opponent wins in round six, and every house
      that met either side of a board has its rung three moved by that board.
C083  A BYE MAKES NO MEETING (C059), so a house lifted by a bye lifts nobody's strength. This is the
      only way a house gains a win without raising the strength of anybody who has met it.
C084  THE RECEIVER OF A CONCESSION MEETS NOBODY (C065), so a concession raises the giver's strength
      by the receiver's wins and raises the receiver's strength by nothing.
C085  A STRUCK HOUSE GOES ON FEEDING STRENGTH. Its boards stand (C070), so its meetings stand, and
      every win it stands on still counts toward the strength of every house that met it. Its own
      strength is still read the same way, off the houses it met, and goes on moving as they win.
C086  RUNG FOUR IS THE OPENING SEED, AND THE WORSE SEED RANKS ABOVE. The house with the HIGHER SEED
      NUMBER ranks above the house with the lower. It is weighed only where two houses hold the
      same wins, the same losses and the same strength.
C087  HEAD TO HEAD IS NEVER WEIGHED. That one house beat another at some board settles nothing about
      the order between them, on any rung, at any moment.
C088  NO TWO HOUSES CAN BE LEVEL ON ALL FOUR RUNGS, because no two houses share an opening seed
      (C013). Rung four therefore always settles, and there is no fifth rung to invent.
C089  A STANDING RANKS EXACTLY THE HOUSES STANDING IN A BERTH AND STILL IN THE CROWN. Nothing else
      is in it.
C090  A HOUSE STANDING IN NO BERTH IS IN NO STANDING, however many wins it holds. A house the record
      never seats is therefore never ranked, never drawn a bye, and never put out by a close.
C091  A HOUSE THAT IS OUT IS IN NO STANDING, whether it was struck, cut or felled.
C092  A STANDING IS TAKEN AT THE STEP THAT READS IT, over the houses seated at that moment and on
      the wins, losses and strength standing at that moment. It is never taken at the close on
      behalf of an earlier step, and never taken at the end of the record on behalf of anything.
C093  THREE THINGS READ A STANDING: a bye, to find the lowest house still seated (C057); a board, to
      know whether the house that took it stood BELOW the house it beat (C129); and a close, to rank
      the field, cut it and reseat it (C101).
C094  THE FROZEN STANDING. At the moment a house goes out, whether struck, cut or felled, its four
      rung values are read one last time and FROZEN: its wins, its losses, its strength as it stands
      at that step, and its seed.
C095  A FROZEN STANDING IS NEVER READ AGAIN FROM THE HOUSE. However the crown moves after it, the
      frozen values stand exactly as they were at the step the house went out, and they are what the
      places are settled on (C143).
C096  A HOUSE THAT IS NEVER PUT OUT has no frozen standing, and where the places are settled it is
      weighed on its four rungs as they stand at the close.
C097  THE STRENGTH COLUMN OF THE CLOSING SHEET IS READ AT THE CLOSE, over the houses that house met,
      on the wins those houses stand on at the close. It is not the frozen strength, so a house put
      out early can print a strength larger than the one its place was settled on.
C098  NO STANDING READS THE BRACKET ORDER, THE ROLL ORDER OR THE ORDER OF THE CLOSING SHEET. The
      four rungs are the whole of it.
