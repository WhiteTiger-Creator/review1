50 - THE STATES, THE TOKENS AND THE PLACES        C127 - C146
==============================================================

C127  EVERY BERTH THE BRACKET NAMES TAKES EXACTLY ONE STATE OUT OF FIVE, weighed in this order, the
      FIRST THAT FITS deciding:
        CROWNED   the house it seats at the close took the crown
        UPSET     a house it seated beat one standing above it
        RESTED    it drew a bye in some round
        VACATED   it seated a house at some time and is not one of the above
        DARK      it never seated a house
      A berth that fits more than one takes the one standing highest here, so the crown's berth is
      CROWNED even where it also hosted an upset and a bye, and a berth that hosted an upset is
      UPSET even where it also drew a bye.
C128  CROWNED takes one of two tokens: held.long where the berth has seated MORE THAN ONE house
      across the crown, then held.own where it has seated exactly one. A berth that seated the same
      house twice running has seated one house (C120).
C129  UPSET. A berth is UPSET where a house sitting in it TOOK A BOARD FROM A HOUSE STANDING ABOVE
      IT, the two being weighed on the standing taken JUST BEFORE THAT BOARD WAS RULED (C093), over
      the field as it stood then. The berth marked is the berth OF THE HOUSE THAT TOOK THE BOARD.
      Where the house that took the board already stood above the house it beat, no berth is marked.
      A concession is never an upset, because a concession takes no board (C064).
C130  UPSET takes one of two tokens, weighed in this order: upset.wide where the beaten house stood
      TWO OR MORE PLACES ABOVE the house that beat it, then upset.close where it stood exactly one
      place above. Places are counted along THAT SAME STANDING, best first: two houses next to each
      other in it stand one place apart, and a house with two others between them stands three
      places above.
C131  THE FIRST UPSET A BERTH HOSTS NAMES IT. Where a berth hosts a second upset later, of either
      width, the mark stands as the first upset set it and is not replaced.
C132  RESTED. A berth is RESTED where a house sitting in it DREW A BYE (C057). The berth marked is
      the berth the house stood in when the bye fell, and THE FIRST BYE A BERTH DRAWS NAMES IT: a
      later bye at the same berth does not replace the mark.
C133  RESTED takes one of two tokens, settled when the bye falls and read off THE SAME STANDING THAT
      PLACED IT, before the bye's win is entered: rest.tie where the house the bye fell on and the
      house ranked immediately above it were LEVEL ON WINS, ON LOSSES AND ON STRENGTH, so that only
      the seed rung separated them, and rest.clear in every other case, including where the bye fell
      on the only house seated.
C134  VACATED, AND THE VACANCY MARK. A berth carries a VACANCY MARK recording HOW THE LAST HOUSE TO
      LEAVE IT LEFT. The mark is set when the berth empties and is discarded the moment the berth
      seats a house again (C119). A VACATED berth takes one of five tokens, weighed in this order,
      the first that fits deciding:
        void.barred     the last house to leave it was STRUCK from the roll
        void.felled     the last house to leave it was FELLED at a close
        void.cut        the last house to leave it was CUT at a close
        void.reseated   a close moved the last house to leave it into another berth (C118)
        void.standing   no house has ever left it: a house is still sitting in it at the close
      A house going out sets the mark for the berth IT WAS SITTING IN, replacing whatever mark that
      berth carried before. A strike of a house standing in no berth empties no berth and sets no
      mark anywhere.
C135  DARK takes one of two tokens: dark.named where some step of the record NAMED the berth (C035)
      although no house was ever seated in it, then dark.silent where no step ever named it.
C136  EVERY HOUSE THE ROLL NAMES TAKES EXACTLY ONE STATE OUT OF FIVE, weighed in this order, the
      FIRST THAT FITS deciding:
        CROWNED   it took the crown
        BARRED    it was struck from the roll
        CUT       a close put it out and it lost no board in the round just closed
        FELLED    a close put it out and it lost the board that put it out
        SEATED    the record closes with it still in the crown
      The crowned house is still sitting in a berth when the record closes, and it reads CROWNED and
      not SEATED because CROWNED is weighed first. A house struck at a close that would also have
      cut it reads BARRED, because BARRED is weighed above CUT and the strike reached it first.
C137  CROWNED takes crown.width or crown.sole as C122 settles it when the crown is taken.
C138  BARRED takes one of two tokens: barred.seated where the house was STANDING IN A BERTH when it
      was struck, and barred.aside where it was standing in no berth at all.
C139  CUT takes one of two tokens, weighed in this order: cut.idle where the house stood in NO BOARD
      at all in the round just closed (C111), then cut.width where it stood in at least one and lost
      none (C112).
C140  FELLED takes one of two tokens, weighed in this order: felled.given where the loss was a board
      the house GAVE UP in the round just closed (C109), then felled.board where it was beaten
      (C110).
C141  SEATED takes one of two tokens: stand.seated where the house IS SITTING IN A BERTH when the
      record closes, and stand.unseated where it is not, which covers a house the record never
      seated and a house a reseating left no berth.
C142  THE PLACES. Every house the roll names takes exactly one PLACE, counting from one, and no two
      houses share a place. WHERE A HOUSE TOOK THE CROWN IT TAKES PLACE ONE OUTRIGHT, whatever its
      standing, and is lifted out of the comparison that settles the rest.
C143  EVERY OTHER HOUSE IS PLACED ON THE STANDING IT HELD WHEN IT WENT OUT, BEST FIRST, weighed on
      the four rungs in their stated order (C072) against the frozen values of C094. A house struck
      or cut early therefore places above a house that survived four more rounds but stood lower
      when it stopped, and the round a house went out in is read for nothing.
C144  A HOUSE NEVER PUT OUT is weighed there on its four rungs as they stand at the close (C096),
      against the frozen values of the houses that went out, exactly as though it had gone out at
      the last step.
C145  THE PLACE TOKEN NAMES THE RUNG THAT SEPARATED A HOUSE FROM THE HOUSE PLACED IMMEDIATELY ABOVE
      IT: place.wins, place.losses, place.strength or place.seed, weighed down the four rungs in
      their stated order and taking the first rung on which the two differ. Where the two are level
      on all four the token is place.seed.
C146  THE HOUSE AT PLACE ONE takes place.crown where it is the crowned house, and where it is the
      only house on the roll. WHERE NO HOUSE TOOK THE CROWN, the house at place one has no house
      above it, so it takes the rung that separates it from the house placed IMMEDIATELY BELOW it,
      by the same four rungs in the same order.
