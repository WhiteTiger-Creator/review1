40 - THE CLOSE                                    C099 - C126
==============================================================

C099  close. The step reads `close` and carries no argument. It ends the round now running and
      opens the next one.
C100  A CLOSE DOES SIX THINGS, IN THIS ORDER AND NO OTHER:
        first    it ranks every house standing in a berth and still in the crown (C101)
        second   it reads the width the NEXT round allows (C102)
        third    it puts out every ranked house standing below that width (C103)
        fourth   it reseats the houses that remain, by rank, into the berths the bracket names
                 first (C115)
        fifth    it crowns the house left alone, where exactly one remains (C121)
        sixth    it advances the round and clears the round marks (C113, C124)
      Nothing in the close is weighed twice and nothing else happens in it at all (C126).
C101  THE RANKING is a standing (C072) taken at this step over the houses seated at this step. It
      is taken once and everything the close does after it reads that one ranking.
C102  THE WIDTH A CLOSE READS is the width of the ROUND IT OPENS, not the round it ends. A close
      ending round three reads the width declared for round four.
C103  EVERY RANKED HOUSE STANDING BELOW THAT WIDTH GOES OUT. Where the width is five, the five best
      ranked houses remain and every house ranked sixth or lower goes out. A house goes out AT A
      CLOSE and never at a board: losing a board does not by itself send a house home.
C104  WHERE THE WIDTH IS AT OR ABOVE THE NUMBER OF HOUSES SEATED, NOBODY GOES OUT, and every house
      that remains is STILL RESEATED by rank into the berths the bracket names first (C115). A close
      that puts nobody out is not a close that does nothing.
C105  A CLOSE WITH NO HOUSE SEATED AT ALL is recorded, puts nobody out, crowns nobody, reseats
      nobody, and still advances the round and clears the round marks.
C106  A CLOSE ASKING FOR A ROUND PAST THE LAST ROUND widths.table declares holds the LAST WIDTH
      DECLARED, for that close and for every close after it (C010).
C107  A HOUSE PUT OUT AT A CLOSE IS EITHER CUT OR FELLED, and the two are separated by ONE QUESTION
      ONLY: did it lose a board IN THE ROUND JUST CLOSED? It did makes it FELLED; it did not makes
      it CUT. Nothing else settles it, and neither its cumulative wins nor its cumulative losses
      settles it.
C108  THE ROUND MARKS are the three marks a step sets on a house: it STOOD IN A BOARD, it LOST A
      BOARD, it GAVE A BOARD UP. C055, C062 and C068 say what sets them. They are the only record
      kept per round and they are read by C109 through C112 and by nothing else.
C109  A house put out that GAVE A BOARD UP in the round just closed is FELLED with felled.given.
      This is weighed FIRST, so a house that both gave a board up and lost another in that round is
      felled.given and never felled.board.
C110  A house put out that did not give a board up but LOST A BOARD in the round just closed is
      FELLED with felled.board.
C111  A house put out that lost no board in the round just closed and STOOD IN NO BOARD in it at all
      is CUT with cut.idle.
C112  A house put out that lost no board in the round just closed and STOOD IN AT LEAST ONE is CUT
      with cut.width. So a house that lost three boards in earlier rounds and won the one board of
      the round just closed is CUT and not FELLED.
C113  THE ROUND MARKS ARE CLEARED AT EVERY CLOSE, after the close has read them. They live for
      exactly one round. A mark set in round two is gone by the time the close of round three reads
      the marks, and no clause anywhere reads a mark from an earlier round.
C114  THE PLACES ARE NOT THE MARKS. The frozen standing (C094) is kept for the whole crown and is
      never cleared; the round marks are cleared at every close and are never kept. The two are read
      within a few steps of each other and are not the same thing.
C115  THE RESEAT. The houses that remain take berths BY RANK INTO BRACKET ORDER: the house ranked
      first takes the berth bracket.table names FIRST, the house ranked second takes the berth it
      names second, and so on down the ranking.
C116  THE RESEAT RUNS AS FAR AS THERE ARE HOUSES REMAINING and no further. Where four houses remain,
      the first four berths of bracket order seat them.
C117  EVERY OTHER BERTH OF THE BRACKET STANDS EMPTY after a reseat, whatever house stood in it
      before. A house is never left in the berth it happened to hold, and a berth is never held
      back for the house that was in it.
C118  A BERTH LEFT EMPTY BY A RESEAT, which has seated a house at some time and carries no vacancy
      mark, takes the vacancy mark void.reseated (C134).
C119  A BERTH SEATED AGAIN DISCARDS ITS VACANCY MARK at once. A berth emptied in round two and
      seated again in round three carries no mark from round two.
C120  A HOUSE MAY BE RESEATED INTO THE BERTH IT ALREADY HELD, where its rank and that berth's place
      in bracket order agree. That berth has then seated the same house twice running, which counts
      as ONE house seated for held.long and held.own (C128).
C121  THE CROWN. Where a close leaves EXACTLY ONE HOUSE remaining, that house takes the crown at
      that close.
C122  THE CROWN'S TWO TOKENS are weighed in this order: crown.width where that same close put at
      least one house out, then crown.sole where it put nobody out.
C123  THE CROWN IS NEVER LOST. A house that has taken the crown keeps it for the rest of the record,
      whatever any later step does, INCLUDING A STRIKE NAMING IT: the crowned house reads CROWNED
      because CROWNED is weighed first on the house ladder (C136).
C124  THE ROUND ADVANCES AT EVERY CLOSE. The record opens standing in round one and every close, of
      whatever kind and whatever it did, moves it on by one. The round the record stands in when it
      ends is what the head line prints (C149).
C125  A HOUSE PUT OUT IS OUT FOR GOOD. No later seat reaches it (C040), no later board or concession
      reaches its berth through it (C045, C067), no later close ranks it (C091), and a later strike
      naming it changes nothing (C070).
C126  NOTHING ELSE HAPPENS AT A CLOSE. No board is weighed again, no bye falls, no seat is
      reconsidered, no hand moves, no meeting is made or unmade, and no house's wins, losses or
      strength change by the closing itself.
