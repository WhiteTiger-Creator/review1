THE LAWS OF THE CROWN
=====================

Six files carry the whole law of the crown. Every rule is numbered, C001 through C160, with no gap
and no number used twice. This index is a map by topic and carries no rule of its own.

  10-the-bracket.txt    C001 - C028
      where the recorded crown is read, and how each of its four tables is read
      the bracket and BRACKET ORDER, which is the reseat order and nothing else
      the widths, one to a round, and the width past the last round declared
      the roll, ROLL ORDER, and the opening seeds
      comments, blank lines, whole numbers, and what the record may never grow

  20-the-record.txt     C029 - C070
      the record read top line first, one numbered step at a time
      the six step kinds and no others
      seat, and the five ways it reaches nothing
      board, the hands, the more hands taking it, and equal hands
      what a board enters, and THE MEETING it makes
      bye, which carries no argument, and what it does not enter
      concede and its asymmetry
      strike, and the boards of a struck house that stand

  30-the-standing.txt   C071 - C098
      the four rungs of the standing, in order
      wins counting byes, losses counting boards given up
      STRENGTH: what it is read off, and that it is read at the moment it is weighed
      the worse seed ranking above, and head to head never weighed
      when a standing is taken, and when it is FROZEN

  40-the-close.txt      C099 - C126
      the close, the ranking it takes, and the width the next round allows
      who goes out, and CUT against FELLED
      the round marks, what sets them and when they are cleared
      THE RESEAT into the berths the bracket names first
      the crown, its two tokens, and that it is never lost

  50-the-states.txt     C127 - C146
      the five berth states, the order they are weighed in, and their tokens
      the five house states, the order they are weighed in, and their tokens
      the places, and the place tokens

  60-the-sheet.txt      C147 - C160
      the --selfcheck flag and what it does not prove
      the closing sheet: the head line, the two blocks, the columns, the two sorts and the
      two tallies, all fixed by C148 through C157
      the running discipline
      the awkward cases of the crown gathered again in one place

Where two rules could be read as touching the same question, the more particular rule governs.
Every rule carrying an order of its own states that order, and the direction of that order, where
it stands.
