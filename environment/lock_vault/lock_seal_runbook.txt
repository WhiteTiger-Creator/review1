Lock Vault release runbook

Symptom. The delivered custody engine and mixed policy do not safely authorize
encrypted PKCS8 release. Both custody surfaces select unsafe behavior in most
families and must be evaluated against the Canon rather than trusted as examples.

Edit allowed. Change /app/lock_lane/main.go, /app/lock_lane/go.mod, and
/app/lock_vault/custody_policy.yaml. Both engine and sheet are graded.

Forbidden. Do not change bags, phrases, iteration policy, Canon, schema,
binding register, mapping, probe desk, sample, or output expectations anywhere
below /app/lock_vault. Do not change /tests, /solution, or
/logs/verifier/reward.txt.

Build and run. Rebuild exactly with:
CGO_ENABLED=0 /usr/local/go/bin/go build -C /app/lock_lane -o /app/bin/lock-release
Then run /app/bin/lock-release with no arguments. Grading performs the rebuild
again, so replacing only /app/bin/lock-release is ineffective.

Outputs. Defaults are /app/output/unwrap_log.jsonl and
/app/output/vault_stamp.txt. A successful complete batch exits zero and writes
nothing to stdout or stderr. The stamp file is exactly three newline-terminated
lines unwrapped=N, denied=N, and stamp=H where H is the labeled sixteen-hex
digest required by the Canon.

Runtime swaps. Nonempty LOCK_VAULT_ROOT, LOCK_LOG_PATH, LOCK_STAMP_PATH, and
LOCK_POLICY replace the default vault, log, stamp, and policy paths.

Grading fixtures. The main vault and /app/lock_vault/probe_desk are exercised,
along with generated bags covering low floors, algorithm order, failed unwrap
burn, repeated encrypted DER, distinct DER for one public key, inclusive hold
windows that do not consume blocked bags, every companion/root policy flip,
recursive clearing, and exact stamp bytes. Several properties may occur in one
generated batch. Probe expected files are immutable observations, not policy
authority.

Compact JSON. Emit one object per processed nonempty line without spaces.
Denied key order is seq, verdict, slot. Accepted key order is seq, verdict,
slot, fp. A nonempty log ends with exactly one newline.

Authority. Read /app/lock_vault/lock_canon.txt, then match its required
behaviors through /app/lock_vault/closed_token_binding.md and the closed values
in /app/lock_vault/policy_schema.md. /app/lock_vault/policy_mapping.md helps
locate families. /app/lock_vault/custody_token_charter.md only restates that
selectors are opaque and never derived. /app/lock_vault/sample is a non-graded
walkthrough and never overrides the Canon. Desk notes are deliberately
unreliable.
