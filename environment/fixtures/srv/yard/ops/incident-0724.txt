INCIDENT 2026-07-24 -- steward controller storage failure

The steward controller's storage array failed during shift S45. The
controller was emergency-reprovisioned on spare hardware the same shift.
Yard power was cycled for the swap: all load stopped 4:23 into S45 and
resumed 4:40. Lost with the array: the steward progress database (per-host
qualified-hour balances; sole copy) and the centralized service logs.
The switchboard roster and calendars, and the exporter accounting on the
collector, are unaffected. The rebuilt steward is running with a re-synced
roster, an empty progress table, and its intake gate closed pending
disposition of the current cohort. Load shifts resumed on schedule from
the restart; the exporter has confirmed normal delivery since.
