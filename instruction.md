Gauntlet is a contest settled by a fixed opponent called the Warden. Each round you and a rival each bring six contenders, described only by their power, guile and armour. You send in one champion, it fights every one of the rival's six contenders in turn, and the Warden decides each of those bouts by one fixed hidden rule. You are judged on how many of the six bouts your champion takes, so the whole round turns on knowing which of your contenders the Warden's rule favours against that particular rival roster. You are given 110 recorded bouts in /app/logs.json, each naming the two contenders and which of them won and nothing else, and the rule can be worked out from them; /app/README.md describes the formats. The Warden does not weigh each contender on its own merits alone: a bout can turn on how one contender's trait measures up against a particular trait of the other, which is why no single ranking of the contenders will ever account for all the results.

Implement chooseChampion(round) in /app/strategy.js: given an object with a mine array and a rivals array of six contenders each, return the index into mine of the champion you send in. Your strategy is judged on held-out rounds that are not shown to you, and a round counts when the champion you send wins as many of the six bouts as the best contender in your roster could have won. You are marked on how many of the thirty rounds you settle that way, and the top mark needs at least twenty-four of them, so a rough sense of which contenders are strong will not carry you. The file /app/cases.json holds practice rounds to experiment with, and node /app/run.js applies your strategy to them. Work in plain JavaScript with no network access.
