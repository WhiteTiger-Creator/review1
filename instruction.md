The tablet team needs a handwritten-digit model for a new stylus sampling policy. Records are real pen trajectories compressed to eight ordered x,y coordinate pairs. Training rows include the digit label. Evaluation rows use the released writer-independent split and omit it, so the model must generalize across unseen writers. Rows also carry capture_action, behavior_propensity, target_probability, and policy_weight.

The public data live under /app/data, and /app/docs/contract.md defines columns, policy fields, metrics, and output rules. Produce /app/outputs/digit_probabilities.csv with exactly record_id, p_0, p_1, p_2, p_3, p_4, p_5, p_6, p_7, p_8, and p_9 columns. Every evaluation record_id must appear exactly once and no extra ids may appear. Probabilities must be finite, between zero and one, and sum to one within 0.000001.

Verifier-held labels score policy-weighted multiclass log loss, macro recall, and Brier score. Overall thresholds are 0.140, 0.955, and 0.060; behavior_propensity <= 0.4 rows 0.100, 0.965, and 0.035; compact_trace rows 0.180, 0.930, and 0.080; digits 1, 5, 7, and 9 rows 0.170, 0.935, and 0.075, and their compact_trace subset 0.245, 0.905, and 0.110. Recall breaks ties toward lower digits. Predictions must vary by record, and keyed probabilities must be stable if evaluation rows are reordered. The project runs offline in R. /app/run.sh accepts optional input and output directories or defaults to /app/data and /app/outputs. The starter analysis is neutral.
