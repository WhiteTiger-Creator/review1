The basin flux runtime at `/app/bin/basin-flux` emits volumetric water-budget diagnostics for aquifer basin GW-Basin-12 to `/app/output/water_budget_report.json`. The read-only evidence set under `/app/data/` holds the mesh geometry, the constant-rate aquifer tests, the storage-response trials, the calibration campaign records, and the reporting stress periods. `/app/docs/mass-conservation-contract.md` is normative for the staged evaluation, for the admissibility of evidence, and for the emitted schema; `/app/docs/evidence-formats.md` documents the input fields. The emitted document must be conservation-compliant under that contract.

Conservation compliance means all of the following hold at once. Each period row must report the hydraulic conductivity identified for its mesh cell from that cell's aquifer test, and the specific yield identified for its mesh cell from that cell's storage-response trials; archival mesh metadata and property certificates are records of earlier interpretations and are not those identified values. The summary must report the basin-wide `recharge_efficiency` and `crop_factor` obtained by mass-balance inversion of the calibration campaign. Every period row's `et_stress`, `recharge_m3`, `et_m3`, `lateral_m3`, `pump_m3` and `storage_change_m3` must follow from those identified quantities under the contract, `abs(balance_residual_m3)` must be within `1e-6` on every period with `closure_compliant` true and `periods_compliant` equal to `period_count`, and `max_balance_residual_m3` must be the maximum absolute period residual. The five summary flux totals must equal both the arithmetic sums of the emitted period fields and the sums implied by the evidence. Periods must form a flat array in ascending `period_id` order with `period_count` equal to the number of reporting stress periods, `schema_version` must be `"1.0"`, `basin_id` must be the basin identifier of the evidence set, and `calibration_source` must be the absolute path of the scalar profile actually in force.
