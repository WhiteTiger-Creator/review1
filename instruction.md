An automotive body plant pushes bare steel shells through a cathodic electrocoat tank and a bake oven. Most come out clean. Now and then one leaves cratered, its film drawn back from a fleck of oil, and nothing in the log records why. Sensors and a few build traits are logged per shell, and the plant wants each one called cratered or clean from those readings alone.

Each row of /app/data/train.csv is one coated shell: six tank sensor readings, three categorical build traits, a bath_run group tag, and a split_role column marking the row fit, validation, or test. The cratered label is filled in for the fit and validation rows and left blank for the test rows you must call. Write /app/analysis.R so that Rscript /app/analysis.R trains and evaluates a k-nearest-neighbour classifier: draw imputation and scaling statistics from fit rows only, choose k from the /app/config/model_config.csv grid by validation macro-F1, refit that k on fit plus validation rows, fix the operating threshold from /app/config/costs.csv, and label the test shells with base R plus jsonlite and no network.

Write every table named in /app/config/report_contract.csv into /app/outputs/, using that exact path, headers, and order; /app/docs/model-contract.md pins the full schema, artifact semantics, floors, and the environment variables that may relocate those paths on rerun. Your labels are checked against withheld true labels. The report tables are recomputed from your serialized probabilities, with no iterative feedback and no credit for restating your own numbers. Clear the accuracy and macro-F1 floors.
