Coastal plastics SNR field desk lives under `/app/environment`. Primary and held-out corpora sit under `/app/environment/corp/primary` and `/app/environment/corp/hold`. When campaign journal seeds already exist under `/app/environment/var/journal`, held-out fatigue algebra and class ladders must still match the published obligations, and the rights mapping sheet plus transparency listing must stay consistent with those regenerated observation roots.

Run `bash /app/environment/drive_k4.sh` so it regenerates `/app/output/obs_primary.json`, `/app/output/obs_hold.json`, `/app/output/rights_map.json`, and `/app/output/transparency.txt`. That command must overwrite all four artifacts on each run. Rewriting only the driver script is not enough, and hand-written files under `/app/output` are not enough; the observation and rights pipeline under `/app/environment` has to produce the correct scientific outputs.

Public obligations live in `/app/environment/docs/pol_n.rst`, tolerances in `/app/environment/docs/tol_n.rst`, and sheet/observation schema in `/app/environment/docs/sch_n.txt`. The rights mapping sheet must keep its `version` field as the fixed token `k4-1` (the five-character k4 hyphen 1 label from the schema notes). Optional argv: `bash /app/environment/drive_k4.sh --help` and `bash /app/environment/drive_k4.sh --root /app/environment --out /app/output`.
