The object-archive pack under `/app/environment` builds `layer_emit` and `yseal`. Rebuilt artifacts currently disagree with the published pack and link-graph contracts on generation refresh, live unit provenance against the object cache, archive-index binding, torn-journal recovery, carry/fence bookkeeping, and the span tag that binds those surfaces together. Restore the pack under `/app/environment` so the rebuilt binaries and the packing chain agree with those contracts.

Regenerate through `/app/environment/tools/run_emit_chain.sh`, which rebuilds, runs `layer_emit`, then runs `yseal`, and writes `/app/output/span.journal` and `/app/output/span_transcript.json`. Those outputs are always produced by the chain from the live inputs; static or manual writes of the journal or transcript are insufficient and are not a fix. A direct seal check invokes `/app/environment/build/cmd/yseal/yseal --journal /app/output/span.journal --report /app/output/span_transcript.json`.

The journal is a little-endian `SPJ2` record carrying gen_epoch, unit_count, per-unit nbytes plus raw bytes, probe_sum, unit_digest, pair_ref, wal_crc, and trailer_gen. The transcript is JSON exposing pair_ref, gen_epoch, mesh_id, unit_digest, seal_class, wal_crc, unit_count, probe_sum, stamp_epoch, carry_hex, span_tag, and index_hex with stable types, where seal_class is one of `mesh_hold_t` or `mesh_open_t`. Field formulas, the SPJ2 byte layout, generation refresh against `/app/environment/data/gen_limit.toml`, content-identity staging versus `/app/environment/var/object_cache/`, archive index rules for `/app/environment/var/ar.index`, carry and fence agreement on index digests, span_tag binding, numeric tolerance, and the open/hold rule are normative in `/app/environment/docs/pack_rules.md` and `/app/environment/docs/pack_contract.json`. Target edges, INTERFACE pollution rules, and pack-configure constraints are normative in `/app/environment/docs/link_graph.md`.
