This is a scientific-computing task in discrete dynamical systems and geometric path-integral analysis on a classification-yard spatial graph under /app. Model occupancy as a constrained discrete-time dynamical process whose admissible states obey the dual physical inequalities n_cars(t) <= max_cars(t) and sum of car length_units on t <= max_length_units(t). After failed-switch edge deletion, verify residual-graph locomotive closure to LEAD by reachability search. The scalar path-integral observable total_distance_m is the sum of edge length_m along PUSH, PULL, and MOVE_LOCO moves, not a hop count.

Compute the sealed occupancy-dynamics atlas: the capacity-feasible move sequence, residual closure verification, destination-block occupancy partitions that preserve inbound order, and path-integral length accounting. The governing physical models and measurement contracts are stated in /app/docs/yod-model.md, /app/docs/yard-physics.md, /app/docs/occupancy-inequalities.md, /app/docs/distance-metric.md, /app/docs/residual-reachability.md, /app/docs/staging-schema.md, /app/docs/export-schema.md, and /app/docs/cli.md.

Yard geometry and consist observations are read from /app/yard-alpha unless HFSY_YARD_DIR names an alternate absolute observation directory. Required scientific outputs are these absolute paths: /app/state/ingest-snapshot.json, /app/state/yard-staging.json, /app/state/shunting-validated.json, /app/state/export-manifest.json, /app/state/export-ledger.json, and /app/output/shunting-sequence.json (plus any seals those files carry). Unchanged inputs must reproduce stable staging digests and byte-stable export fingerprints under the YOD physics contracts, while each successful export still appends a new ledger entry. Graded physics includes both capacity inequalities on the live OccupancyGuard and optimize replay, residual BFS closure with LEAD as a reachability target rather than a required end track, geometric length_m path measure, and capacity_verified false whenever an emitted THROW_SWITCH targets a failed switch.
