FALLOWFIELD COMMISSIONING YARD -- INTAKE STANDARD (IS-11, in force)

Qualification. A host graduates when it has banked at least 120 qualified
hours in each load class (hammer, carousel, flood). An hour is qualified
only if it is a full wall-clock hour during which the host's pod was under
all-three-class concurrent load and the host was in load phase. Partial
hours do not count, in either direction. A pod receiving partial or
degraded delivery of any class is not under load for qualification
purposes. Hosts not in load phase accrue nothing. Shifts are 8 hours,
hour-aligned, three per day, continuous. Phase transitions occur only at
shift boundaries.

Yard limit. A host must complete qualification no later than the 54th
shift counting from and including its racking shift. A host that cannot
complete within its limit restarts intake from zero. There is no
adjustment of the limit for equipment faults, schedule gaps, or yard
incidents, whatever their cause.

Continuation slots. A continuing host is assigned the shifts it will run,
starting from the earliest future shift, skipping that host's planned
reimage shifts, at most 8 hosts of the same pod per shift. When more than
8 same-pod hosts need a shift, hosts with more remaining hours go first;
equal remainders order by host id. A host's final slot is the shift in
which its remaining requirement is met.

Boundaries. "At least 120" is inclusive. "No later than the 54th shift"
is inclusive: a final slot equal to the limit completes in time; one past
it does not.

Placement. A graduating host enters its pod's production cell at the next
free position in rack order.

IS-11 supersedes IS-10 (carousel co-ration clause removed).   -- Yard eng.
