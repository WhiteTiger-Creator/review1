CS-9 CAROUSEL FEEDER -- applications note (vendor sheet, p.4 extract)

Each CS-9 unit feeds one pod. Delivery confirmation integrates with the
yard scheduler per minute of drive. Fault behaviour: CS-9 degradation
faults are latching. A unit that enters degraded drive delivers no
qualifying load until it is power-cycled; there is no in-place recovery
and no partial re-arm. After a power cycle the unit re-arms cold and
resumes confirmed delivery immediately. Scheduled service interval 400h.
